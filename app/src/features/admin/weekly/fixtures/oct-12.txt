October 12th, 2026 – Weekly Kingdom Prayers
1. Father, let the heavens be opened over every minister and speaker during Mega Exploits for
unusual revelations, imparting grace, wisdom, and understanding to all participants, according to
Acts 19:20, which says, “So mightily grew the word of God and prevailed.” in the name of
Jesus Christ.
2. Father, give us an encounter with Your prophetic Word that will open our destiny during Mega
Exploits – according to Psalm 105:19, which says, “Until the time that his word came: the
word of the LORD tried him. The king sent and loosed him; even the ruler of the
people, and let him go free.” In the name of Jesus Christ.
3. Father, let the theme for Mega Exploits 2026, The Chief Cornerstone, answer to its name in
the lives of every participant – according to Genesis 2:19, which says, “…and whatsoever
Adam called every living creature, that was the name thereof.” in Jesus Christ name.
4. Father, by the blood of Jesus, we decree peace across the length and breadth of our nation during
and after God's Remnant Assembly Mega Exploits 2026 – according to Job 34:29 which says,
“When he giveth quietness, who then can make trouble? and when he hideth his face,
who then can behold him? whether it be done against a nation, or against a man
only...” in Jesus Christ name.
5. Father, thank You for making the feet of our father and mother-in-the-Lord and their family like
hinds’ feet; thank You because You have bruised satan under their feet and have made them to
become their footstool – according to Psalms 18:33, Psalms 110:1 & Romans 16:20, which says,
“He maketh my feet like hinds' feet, and setteth me upon my high places…And the God
of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly…Sit thou at my right hand, until I
make thine enemies thy footstool.” In Jesus Christ name.
6. Father, by the blood of Jesus, we sanctify all equipments that shall be used during God's
Remnant Assembly Mega Exploits both in God's Remnant Assembly Campus and for all our
online viewers around the world for excellent and hitch- free performance – according to
Hebrews 9:22 which says, “And almost all things are by the law purged with blood; and
without shedding of blood is no remission.” in Jesus Christ name.
7. Father, we decree seasons of unstoppable advancement and enlargement as an aftermath of Mega
Exploits for God’s Remnant Assembly worldwide, according to Isaiah 54:2-3, which says,
“Enlarge the place of thy tent, and let them stretch forth the curtains of thine
habitations: spare not, lengthen thy cords, and strengthen thy stakes…” in the name of
Jesus Christ.

In Jesus Christ name we pray, AMEN!
